## Notes on individual sources

- **S01**: Called ISER 2008 in the record. State-contract delivery charges (Table 10), barge distances (Table 15), wharfage, air charter and trucking examples.
- **S02**: Called ISER 2010 in the record. Line-haul barge rate, village delivery cost build-up, terminal fees (Table 4), air cost per hundred air miles.
- **S03**: Called DEC 2007. Source of the $0.007 per gallon per map mile river barge figure and the 1.0 to 1.5 cents per gallon per map mile air figure. No public link recorded in the project.
- **S04**: Ocean barge and tanker legs (slides 14-15, read from the slide images) and the Valdez to Fairbanks truck leg.
- **S05**: Transport-cost ranges (page 8), lightering areas and their coordinates, which ports have docks.
- **S06**: Nome inbound and export fuel fees (3.5 and 1.2 cents per gallon), lightering practice. Has no vessel operating costs.
- **S07**: Inbound petroleum wharfage $0.06 per gallon (p. 58) and fuel transfer charge $0.06 per gallon (p. 39). The link is the port's landing page.
- **S08**: Bulk liquid wharfage $0.05 inbound, $0.032 outbound, $0.017 over-side. The link is the port's home page as printed on the tariff.
- **S09**: Item 260 petroleum wharfage and related charges, modernization surcharge.
- **S10**: Earlier edition, kept for comparison with 10.2.
- **S11**: Cost components (PDF pp. 83 and 85) used for a truck-haul cross-check of the road rate.
- **S12**: Marine header $0.15, truck rack $0.25, internal transfer $0.25, storage $2.25 per barrel per month. The only truck-rack figure.
- **S13**: Cost per truck mile and tank-carrier revenue per mile. Contiguous US data, not Alaska.
- **S14**: Delivery charges by location used as a bound on terminal fees.
- **S15**: Utility fuel price and gallons for 171 communities (page text decoded for this work). Used for revealed price differences and ceilings.
- **S16**: General cargo per pound. Not a bulk fuel rate; used as a ceiling for the plane rate.
- **S17**: As S16. Hazardous material fee $70 per shipment.
- **S18**: Sailings per season by carrier and river reach, draft limits, and a row for each of 56 communities saying how fuel and freight arrive. No prices.
- **S19**: Trail distances between villages; North Slope snow roads built at about $5,000 per mile. No haul prices.
- **S20**: Landing description for 111 communities; barging cost of $12,000 to $15,000 per day for construction work (PDF p. 166). The link came from a web search result.
- **S21**: A $4.7 million supplemental budget line for community winter access trails. No fuel haul rate.
- **S22**: Read for the first batch's winter haul fact sheet; see addendum_ice_road.md for what was taken from it.
- **S23**: Two files with identical bytes: NorthSlope_CommunityWinterTrails_2020.pdf and NorthSlopeBorough-communityWinterAccessTrails2020proj62733.pdf.
- **S24**: Two files with identical bytes: Northwest_Arctic_Borough_Noatak_Winter_Fuel_Haul_System.pdf and NorthwestArcticBorough-NoatekWinterFuelHaulSystem.pdf. Capital $350,000 for tractor, sled and shipment plus $75,000 for permitting (image page 5).
- **S25**: A 2014 North Slope Borough estimate of $450 to $600 per cubic yard to haul gravel from Utqiagvik to Atqasuk by ice road (p. 76).
- **S26**: Beaver's fuel is flown in because the Yukon channel moved; barge freight twice a summer from Nenana.
- **S27**: The 2006 consolidated bulk fuel purchase price ($3.07 per gallon heating fuel, $3.15 gasoline) for seven Norton Sound communities. Delivery terms not stated.
- **S28**: Tank farm capacities (Table 7); 'Most communities in the region receive their bulk fuel shipments via barge during the summer'.
- **S29**: A price model with no distance variable, so it gave no per-mile rate.
- **S30**: Says delivery costs are not published for antitrust reasons.
- **S31**: Sensitivity only; never the base inflation index.
- **S32**: Sensitivity for ocean and coastal barge figures.
- **S33**: Sensitivity for river barge figures.
- **S34**: The index basis for the whole record (January to September 2026 average, 332.243). A mirror, not the BLS table itself; replace with the BLS file when available.
- **S35**: Read page by page on 2026-07-15 per chapter 01; NOT among the files supplied in November, so its figures were not re-confirmed.
- **S36**: Used by the earlier barge chapter for sailing distances; not among the supplied files. This record uses model network miles instead.
- **S37**: Checked against primary sources in Fuel Cost Derivation Verification.md; not used as evidence.
- **S38**: Audited against the local documents on 2026-11-17; see chapter 07 section 4 for what held and what did not.

## Two kinds of source that are not evidence

- The two outside deep-research reports (S37, S38) were audited against the primary documents; their claims are not used as evidence.
- Web search snippets found during the work are grade C and were not used as evidence. Where a figure came only from a snippet, the addendum says so.
