# The full Alaska multimodal network — with the new official flight data

Built by the mmnet pipeline (`output/03_network`) with the **new official air-cargo data** now wired in, and
analyzed here: road + ice + waterway + air, connected by the transfer / bridge / connect-to-giant passes.
Generated by `01_multimodal_network.py` + `02_air_role.py`. Research only.

## The network
- **82,376 nodes / 90,956 edges.** Length by mode: **Waterway 31,995 km · Road 28,344 · Air 24,608 ·
  IceRoad 5,699**.
- Connectors: 201 barge transfers (ports + barge hubs), **78 air↔road transfers** (airports), 1,298 welds +
  bridges, 83 connect-to-giant shore landings.

## Connectivity — one multimodal giant
- **67 components; the giant holds 98.6 % of all nodes.**
- Per-mode share reachable in the giant:

| mode | nodes | in giant |
|---|---|---|
| Road | 47,278 | **97.7 %** |
| Waterway | 34,054 | **100 %** |
| Ice Road | 931 | **95.4 %** |
| **Air** | 113 | **100 %** |

- **Fuel hubs: 331 / 384 (86 %) reachable** through the multimodal network.

## What the new air data adds (with vs without air)
Dropping the air edges + air↔road transfers and recomputing the giant isolates air's marginal contribution:

| metric | without air | with air |
|---|---|---|
| components | 214 | **67** |
| giant nodes | 80,131 | **81,257** |
| road % in giant | 95.6 % | **97.7 %** |
| fuel hubs in giant | 287 | **331** |

- **1,126 nodes join the giant ONLY via air** — including **1,013 road nodes and 44 fuel hubs**. Air cuts
  the component count from 214 to 67 and lifts fuel-hub reachability by **+44 hubs**.
- These are the western/northern bush communities (the Bethel/Nome/Kotzebue/Fairbanks/Anchorage fans) that
  have no road, ice, or barge link to the giant — air is their only connection to the network.

## Takeaways
1. With the new official flight data, the multimodal network is **98.6 % connected** in one giant, and
   **86 % of fuel hubs** are reachable.
2. **Air is decisive for the bush:** it connects **44 fuel hubs and ~1,000 road nodes** that road + barge +
   ice cannot reach — without it the network fragments into 214 pieces.
3. Waterway and air are both fully in the giant (100 %); road (97.7 %) and ice (95.4 %) trail only by the
   genuinely remote pieces beyond every policy's reach.
