# Final ice-ice network — raw → noded → welded (giant + disconnected)

Evaluates the ice↔ice connectivity end to end and renders the final ice-only network at three weld
tolerances. Generated by `02_final_network.py` (figure `out/02_ice_final.png`, tables
`out/ice_final_compare.csv` + `out/ice_disconnected_*.csv`, networks `out/ice_final_{450,1000,3000}m__edges.gpkg`).

## How the component count evolves

| stage | components | giant | disconnected |
|---|---|---|---|
| **raw ice lines** (interim, 399 features) | **42** | — | — |
| **noded ice** (built network) | **47** (931 nodes, 1,248 edges) | 728 (78 %) | 46 |
| **weld @ 450 m** | **33** | 756 (81 %) | 32 |
| **weld @ 1 km** | **27** | 764 (82 %) | 26 |
| **weld @ 3 km** | **19** | 792 (85 %) | 18 |

Note raw 42 → noded 47: R's noding splits lines at crossings and does **not** bridge sub-tolerance
cross-segment gaps, so the noded graph has a few *more* pieces than the raw line-touch count. The ice↔ice
weld then reduces them again.

## The final network at each tolerance

| weld tol | weld edges | components | giant | disconnected | 2-node stubs | largest disconnected | longest weld |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 450 m | 16 | 33 | 756 (81 %) | 32 | 24 | 49 | 391 m |
| 1 km | 22 | 27 | 764 (82 %) | 26 | 19 | 61 | 985 m |
| 3 km | 35 | 19 | 792 (85 %) | 18 | 13 | 63 | 2,756 m |

- **Giant component:** one dominant ice system (the North Slope / Barrow trail network), 728 nodes raw,
  growing to **792 nodes (85 %)** at the 3 km weld.
- **Disconnected paths:** the remaining pieces are **mostly 2-node stubs** (24 of 32 at 450 m; 13 of 18 at
  3 km) — short isolated trail segments — plus a few larger separate trail systems (largest disconnected
  ≈ 49–63 nodes). These are genuinely separate winter routes, not noding gaps, so they stay disconnected
  even at 3 km. Per-tolerance lists with sizes + locations are in `ice_disconnected_{tol}m.csv`.

## Final networks (QGIS-openable)

`out/ice_final_{450,1000,3000}m__edges.gpkg` — the ice edges **plus** the ice↔ice weld connectors, each
row tagged `component`, `is_giant`, `source` (`IceRoad` / `weld:IceRoad`) and `length_m`. Style by
`is_giant` to see the giant vs the disconnected paths, or by `source` to see the weld connectors.

## Takeaway

The ice network is **one ~85 % giant + a long tail of small separate trail systems**. The ice↔ice weld
only closes the handful of short branch gaps (16–35 connectors, all ≤ tol); it cannot unify the ice
because the leftover pieces are real separate routes spread across the Slope. Increasing the tolerance
from 450 m → 3 km moves the giant 81 % → 85 % and halves the disconnected count (32 → 18), but the tail
never disappears. Research only — no engine/profile changes.
