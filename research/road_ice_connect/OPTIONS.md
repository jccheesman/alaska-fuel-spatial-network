# North Slope ice ↔ road — connection options (no 55 km edge)

How to connect the North Slope ice network to the road (grey) network without a 55 km fabricated edge.
Road + ice only. Generated by `07_north_slope_options.py` (figure `out/07_north_slope_options.png`, table
`out/north_slope_options.csv`).

## The hard constraint

Routing through **every** ice and road component (oilfield grids included), the best chain from the big
Barrow ice system (728 nodes) to the road backbone has a **bottleneck of 38.6 km** — and at every hop cap
up to 30 km the Barrow ice never reaches the backbone. There is a genuine ~38–55 km empty stretch with
nothing to chain through. So: short proximity links connect the ice to its **local** roads, but the giant
reaches the **main backbone** only across one long edge — the question is which option you accept.

## The options (none uses a 55 km edge)

| option | new edges | longest edge | ice→roads | ice→backbone | components | fabricated long edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — regional 3 km** | 1,462 | ≤ 3 km | **96 %** | 10.7 % | 186 | **no** |
| **B — real winter route\*** | (3 km + route) | short hops | 96 %+ | high\* | — | no (real data) |
| **C — shortest connector** | 2 | 38.6 km | 78 % | 78.4 % | 1,474 | yes (39 km) |
| **D — hybrid (A + connector)** | 1,464 | 38.6 km | **96 %** | **95.4 %** | 184 | yes (1 edge, 39 km) |

\* Option B = the 3 km regional geometry **plus** the real seasonal **Colville River / Nuiqsut ice road**
sourced as data. That oil-industry winter road connects Nuiqsut (on the Colville, ~70.2 N −151 W) to the
Kuparuk/Deadhorse road system. Added as real geometry it would chain the giant in via **short hops** — no
long fabricated edge. Without sourcing the route it equals A.

## What each buys

- **A — regional 3 km (nothing fabricated).** 96 % of ice joins its local roads; the Deadhorse ice reaches
  the Dalton → Fairbanks. The Barrow giant joins its local Barrow grid but stays off the main backbone
  (only 11 % of ice reaches the backbone). Physically honest — Barrow is genuinely road-isolated.
- **B — real winter route.** The physically correct fix: add the Colville/Nuiqsut seasonal ice road. Best
  realism, no fabrication — but requires sourcing that route geometry (external data).
- **C — shortest explicit connector (≈38.6 km).** One `WinterRoute` edge connects the giant to the
  backbone → 78 % of ice reaches it. Far shorter than 55 km, but it is a single long modeled edge and adds
  no local connections on its own.
- **D — hybrid (recommended if a connector is acceptable).** A + the one 38.6 km `WinterRoute` edge:
  **96 % ice→roads and 95 % ice→backbone** — the fullest connection, with the smallest possible single
  long edge (38.6 km, never 55 km).

## Recommendation

- If **no fabricated long edge** is acceptable → **A** (honest; the North Slope ice is a connected regional
  network, not tied to Fairbanks — which is the real geography).
- If the North Slope **must** reach the main backbone → **B** is correct *if* the Colville/Nuiqsut route can
  be sourced; otherwise **D** (one explicit 38.6 km `WinterRoute` edge, clearly tagged as seasonal).
