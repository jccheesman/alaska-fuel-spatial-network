# The Alaska waterway network (from the raw National Waterway Network)

Defines the Alaska waterway network from the raw NWN, replacing the facility-bbox clip (282 edges) in the
built network. Road↔waterway connection is deferred — this just **defines the network**. Uses mmnet as-is:
ports via `mmnet.build._load_anchor("ports", cfg)`, barge hubs from `output/02_hubs.gpkg`. Generated by
`01_ak_waterway.py` (figure `out/01_ak_waterway.png`, networks `out/ak_waterway_{akonly,akspine}__edges.gpkg`).

## From national → Alaska

The raw source is the **National Waterway Network** (6,859 features). Filtered to Alaska by bounds:

| extent | features | vertices | components | giant | total length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — AK-only** (lat>50, AK lon) | 316 | 34,054 | **1** | 100 % | **31,903 km** |
| **B — AK + Pacific spine** (lat≥46, Pacific lon) | 608 | 37,731 | 3 | ~100 % | 39,433 km |

- **AK-only is one fully-connected marine network** — Gulf of Alaska, Cook Inlet, Bering Sea, Arctic Ocean
  deep-water spines + port access, plus the navigable river penetration the NWN carries. No fragmentation
  to fix.
- **AK+spine** adds the ~7,500 km Pacific deep-water spine linking Alaska to the Pacific NW (Seattle/Tacoma
  — where fuel barges originate). Same Alaska coverage; it only extends the supply line southward.

## Port + barge-hub coverage (mmnet data, as-is)

Identical for both extents (the spine is offshore/southward, adds no AK coverage):

| within | ports (of 147) | barge hubs (of 202) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 km | 56 | 55 |
| 5 km | 94 | 107 |
| 20 km | 135 | 151 |

So ~64 % of ports and ~53 % of barge hubs are within 5 km of the waterway; ~92 % / 75 % within 20 km. The
**~25 % of barge hubs beyond 20 km** are river/inland villages the marine NWN does not reach — a known
limit of the source data (no small-river coverage).

## Recommendation

- **A (AK-only)** for "the Alaska waterway network" proper — clean, fully connected, 31,903 km.
- **B (AK+spine)** if the model must represent the barge **supply line from the lower-48** (origin in the
  Pacific NW). Same Alaska coverage, +Pacific spine.

## Outputs (for the later waterway↔road study)

`out/ak_waterway_{akonly,akspine}__edges.gpkg` — the AK waterway lines tagged `component`, `is_giant`,
`length_m`. This is the input network for a follow-up **waterway↔road** connection study, where — kept
coherent with mmnet — **ports are the anchor** (Road↔Barge transfers happen at ports) and the **barge hubs
are the demand**. Defining the network was the goal here; the connection is the next step.
